Tiny trainers

Small single-page trainers for the bits of the ATPL syllabus that reward drilling rather than reading — the tables, the thresholds, the numbers you either know cold or don't. Each one runs entirely offline: no build step, no dependencies. Grouped below by EASA syllabus subject.

No trainer matches that. Clear the filter to see all of them.

Adding a trainer: drop the HTML file in trainers/<code>-<subject>/, link assets/theme.css for the shared chrome, then add one entry to the TRAINERS list at the bottom of this file. Subjects with no trainers stay hidden until they have one.
